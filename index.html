    Power-Ups

    Newton's 1st Law

    At rest stays at rest. Moving keeps moving (same speed, same direction) unless an outside force acts. That laziness is called inertia.

    Newton's 2nd Law

    F = m × a

    More force means more acceleration. More mass means you NEED more force.

    Newton's 3rd Law

    Every action has an equal and opposite reaction. Two objects, same size forces, opposite directions, at the same time. Smaller mass gets the bigger change in motion.

    Momentum

    p = m × v

    Mass in motion, in kg·m/s.

    Kinetic energy

    KE = ½ × m × v²

    Energy of motion. Double the mass, 2× KE. Double the speed, 4× KE (speed is squared).

    Potential energy

    PE = m × g × h

    Stored energy. g = 9.8 m/s² always. Double the height, 2× PE.

    Net force

    Same direction: add. Opposite directions: subtract, and it points toward the bigger one. 5 N → and ← 3 N gives 2 N →. Zero net force = balanced = no change in motion.

    Force crew

    Gravity pulls down. Friction fights motion. Spring scale measures force in newtons.
